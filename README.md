# Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn, retention, customer value and revenue risk using SQL, Python and Power BI. The objective is to identify key churn drivers, understand customer behavior, quantify revenue associated with churn and prioritize customers for retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Objective

- Analyze customer churn and retention patterns
- Identify major churn drivers
- Compare churn across customer segments, contracts, payment methods and tenure groups
- Analyze revenue associated with churn
- Identify high-value churned customers
- Prioritize customers for retention strategies

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard contains 3 pages:

### Page 1 — Customer Overview

Provides an overview of customers, revenue, churn and customer-value distribution.

### Page 2 — Churn & Retention Analysis

Analyzes churn and retention across customer value, contract, payment method and tenure.

### Page 3 — Revenue & Customer Risk

Analyzes revenue exposure, churn-associated revenue, retention priority and high-value at-risk customers.

## 📈 Key Findings

- Analyzed 7,043 customer records.
- Overall churn rate was approximately 27%.
- Low Value customers had the highest churn rate at approximately 39.51%.
- Medium Value customers had a churn rate of approximately 23.72%.
- High Value customers had a churn rate of approximately 16.51%.
- Identified 387 high-value churned customers.
- Analyzed approximately 16.06M in total customer revenue.
- Identified approximately 2.86M in churn-associated revenue.

## 💡 Business Impact

The analysis helps identify high-risk customer groups and high-value churned customers, enabling businesses to prioritize retention efforts and understand potential revenue exposure.
